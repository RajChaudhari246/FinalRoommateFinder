    About RoommateFinder

    Building transparent, safe, and community-driven housing for students and young professionals across India.

    Finding a good flat in college hubs like Shirpur, Pune, Mumbai, or Bengaluru has always been stressful. Traditional brokers charge exorbitant fees and push mismatched roommates together, leading to disputes over guests, study hours, and cleanliness.

    RoommateFinder was created to put people first. By matching on real lifestyle habits, transparent budget boundaries, and verified safety protocols, we help young people turn shared flats into supportive homes.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about finding roommates on RoommateFinder.

    Is RoommateFinder really free from brokerage?

    Yes, 100%. RoommateFinder connects students and young professionals directly with each other. There are zero broker commissions, hidden listing charges, or transaction fees.

    How does the Compatibility Algorithm work?

    Our rule-based engine scores compatibility based on weighted living factors: budget overlap (25%), location proximity (20%), food habits (10%), smoking policy (10%), study rhythm (10%), sleeping hours (10%), and cleanliness standards (10%).

    What makes the Girls Safe Wing special?

    The Girls Wing provides verified female-only roommate listings with explicit safety protocols, including male visitor policies, society gate curfews, 24/7 security guard presence, and emergency guardian verification.

    What happens when I click "Express Interest"?

    The recipient receives an instant notification with your personal note. If they accept your interest, you both unlock mutual connection status and can coordinate shared flat visits.
