Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Roommate Finder.

How do the separate Girls and Boys sections provide extra safety?

Each section features customized questionnaires tailored to safety and lifestyle requirements. In the Girls Safe Wing, questions cover strict visitor policies (e.g. female guests only, no overnight guests), gated perimeter security (CCTV, 24/7 security guard), and curfew hours. In the Boys Living Wing, protocols address study hours, guest policies, and shared amenities. Compatibility scoring factors in mutual security protocol agreement.

How is compatibility calculated?

We use a rule-based algorithm that evaluates budget overlap (25%), location proximity (20%), food preferences (10%), smoking habits (10%), work/study schedule (10%), sleep schedule (10%), cleanliness standards (10%), and household size (5%), plus security protocol alignment.

Are my personal characteristics separate from what I want in a roommate?

Yes! For example, you can be a non-smoker who prefers living with non-smokers, or a meat-eater who is happy rooming with vegetarians. Personal profile attributes and desired criteria are saved independently.

How do I express interest in someone?

Once logged in, click "Express Interest" on any roommate card. You can include a custom note. The recipient will see your request on their Interests page and can accept or decline.
