Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Roommate Finder.

How is compatibility calculated?

We use a rule-based algorithm that evaluates budget overlap (25%), location proximity (20%), food preferences (10%), smoking habits (10%), work/study schedule (10%), sleep schedule (10%), cleanliness standards (10%), and household size (5%).

Are my personal characteristics separate from what I want in a roommate?

Yes! For example, you can be a non-smoker who prefers living with non-smokers, or a meat-eater who is happy rooming with vegetarians. Personal profile attributes and desired criteria are saved independently.

How do I express interest in someone?

Once logged in, click "Express Interest" on any roommate card. You can include a custom note. The recipient will see your request on their Interests page and can accept or decline.
