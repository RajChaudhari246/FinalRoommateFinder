    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about RoommateFinder.

    How do the separate Girls and Boys sections provide extra safety?

    Each section features customized questionnaires tailored to safety and lifestyle requirements. In the Girls Safe Wing, questions cover strict visitor policies (e.g. female guests only, no overnight guests), gated perimeter security (CCTV, 24/7 security guard), and curfew hours. In the Boys Living Wing, protocols address study hours, guest policies, and shared amenities.

    How is compatibility calculated?

    We evaluate budget overlap (25%), location proximity (20%), food preferences (10%), smoking habits (10%), work/study schedule (10%), sleep schedule (10%), cleanliness standards (10%), and household size (5%), plus mutual security protocol agreement.

    How do I express interest in someone?

    Once logged in, click "Express Interest" on any roommate card. You can include a custom note. The recipient will see your request on their Interests page and can accept or decline. Both users can then coordinate flat visits.
